# Allow role-specific entrance shapes

Individual Role Parts are not required to attack at beat zero. The Ambient Session should normally establish material immediately through its combined structural roles, while Bass may use downbeat anchors, delayed entrances, middle calls, bookends, or tail pickups and foreground roles may retain short entrance delays. This preserves nonempty, independently useful clips without forcing every rhythmic identity into the same phrase construction.

## Consequences

Development and Tension Bass parts distribute activity through the complete Scene Cycle, including its final bars. Foundation and Release remain sparse and may finish early or reserve material for the end. A Bass-only matrix may begin with a deliberate rest.
