# Allow delayed foreground entrances

**Status:** Superseded by [ADR 0012](./0012-allow-role-specific-entrance-shapes.md)

Lead and Arp Line may enter within the first four beats instead of sounding at beat zero, while structural roles still establish immediate material and every Role Part remains nonempty. Requiring every lane to attack at launch would synchronize the matrix, homogenize phrase construction, and confuse “never generate a silent clip” with “every clip must sound continuously.”
