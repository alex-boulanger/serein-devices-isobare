# Separate Role Styles from Role Variants

A Role Style is an explicit generation contract within one Musical Role, while a Role Variant is a repeated lane with a complementary Orchestral Identity. Bass initially supports Sustained and Articulated styles, and Lead supports Pluck and Flow; styles are not generalized to every role until listening reveals a genuinely different musical contract.

## Consequences

Role Style is stored in the versioned recipe and selected per lane. Role Families continue to group all styles and variants of the same Musical Role for density budgeting, because alternate styles are normally performance choices rather than instructions to layer every lane.
