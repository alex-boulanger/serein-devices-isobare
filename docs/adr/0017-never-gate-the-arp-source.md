# Never gate the Arp Source

Space opens the sustained roles by releasing their notes early and letting long
holds re-articulate mid-cycle. The Arp Source is exempt. It is sustained pitch
material for Live's Arpeggiator rather than something heard directly, so
releasing it early or chopping it stops the arpeggio instead of opening the
texture.

Space still shapes the Arp Source, but its register rather than its envelope:
voices stack upward with a minimum gap that widens as Space opens, so the
Arpeggiator receives a spread chord instead of a cluster of neighbouring scale
degrees.

## Consequences

The Arp Source also carries no playback probability under ADR 0009. Thinning it
between passes would stall the Arpeggiator mid-pattern, so every note it holds
is guaranteed.

It is likewise exempt from downbeat allocation (ADR 0015). That was learned the
hard way: staggering its entrance behind the Drone left an audible gap at the
top of every clip, because a Scene's whole opening bar arrives through the
Arpeggiator. "Sustained for the whole Scene Cycle" means from beat zero, not
merely uninterrupted once it starts.
