# Bound the Lead by the Harmonic Path, except under high Tension

The Lead was harmonically blind: it read only which Scene it was rendering and
never the Harmonic Path, so roughly one lead note in ten sounded a semitone or
major seventh against a Pad note sounding at that moment. Phrase boundaries are
now pulled onto a tone of the harmony beneath them, and interior notes move by a
scale degree when they would otherwise clash.

Above a Tension threshold the interior is deliberately left alone. Correcting
every clash at every setting would make Tension's consonant-to-abrasive range
mean nothing, so the friction survives exactly where the artist asked for it.

## Consequences

The Melodic Motif keeps its identity: alignment moves a note by at most two
scale degrees, never leaves the project scale, and never touches the rhythm.
